About AI Academy

We are building the next generation of AI engineers and roboticists — one project at a time.

Our Mission

"To make Artificial Intelligence and Robotics accessible, practical, and genuinely exciting for every student aged 16 and above — by teaching them not just to understand technology, but to create it."

🎯
Who We Are

AI Academy is a Bangalore-based specialized learning institution focused exclusively on Artificial Intelligence and Robotics education. We believe the best way to learn is to build — every concept is taught through real-world projects.

🧪
Our Approach

We teach in a structured, two-level program. Level 1 builds your AI foundations. Level 2 takes you into advanced territory — Computer Vision, IoT, 3D Robotics, and Generative AI. Theory and practice, always together.

🌍
Who We Teach

Our program is designed for students between 16 and 22 years old — high schoolers, undergraduates, and recent graduates who are ready to take their future seriously and build skills that matter in 2025 and beyond.

🏆
What Sets Us Apart

No generic slides. No passive lectures. Every session is hands-on, every module ends in a project, and every student leaves with a portfolio of intelligent systems they actually built from scratch.

🔒
Our Commitment to Quality

Our curriculum is proprietary and continuously updated. We keep our full syllabus and teaching methodology exclusive to our students — because quality education deserves to be protected.

📍
